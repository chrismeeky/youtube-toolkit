YouTube Toolkit

Sign-in is required from 1 January 2027

Until now YouTube Toolkit has worked without an account. From 1 January 2027 you will need to sign in to keep using it. Until that date nothing changes.

Why we are doing this

  • Your preferences stay with you. Your settings, templates and display choices are saved to your account, so they survive a new browser, a new computer or a reinstall.
  • Analytics. Accounts let us see which features are used and where people get stuck. The analytics are for us, to help us serve you better; they are not a dashboard for you.
  • Fair use of shared services. Features such as Similar Channels rely on a server that costs money to run. Accounts let us apply sensible per-person limits instead of limiting everyone.

What will not change

  • The extension stays free.
  • Channel and video figures are still fetched from YouTube pages in your own browser.
  • We will not sell your data or show you ads.

What we will store

Your sign-in identity (such as your email address), your saved preferences, and basic usage analytics tied to your account. We will update our privacy policy with the full detail before sign-in starts, and you will be able to ask for your data to be deleted.
